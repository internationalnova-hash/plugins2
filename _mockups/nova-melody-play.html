Mockup — a third tab, Play, for playing melodies without knowing theory. Pick a key (or Auto) and every key you press is a good note: the 8 melody pads (or A–K on your computer keyboard, or any MIDI keyboard) are the notes of the key, numbered 1–8 with what each one feels like. The 7 chord pads play a whole chord with one finger, and while a chord plays, the melody notes that fit it glow. Tap the melody lets you perform a generated melody by tapping any key in your own rhythm. Record what you play — it turns into MIDI you can drag out, and timing can be fixed for you.
